Never Fail Media creates short-form video for @NeverFailMedia on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

Never Fail Media Automation

Never Fail Media Automation is our private, single-creator desktop tool for preparing and publishing our own short videos to our own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other people.

  • We sign in with each platform's official login (OAuth). We never ask for or store passwords.
  • Every TikTok post is reviewed in a posting screen first: video preview, caption, who can view it, interaction settings and commercial-content disclosure. Nothing is posted until we confirm.
  • We only post videos we made ourselves or are authorized to use.
