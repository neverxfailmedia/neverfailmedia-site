Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Who we are

Never Fail Media operates Never Fail Media Automation (the "Tool"), a private desktop application used only to publish Never Fail Media's own videos to its own accounts (@NeverFailMedia).

2. What data the Tool handles

  • Our own account authorization. When we connect our TikTok account, TikTok issues access and refresh tokens. They are stored only on our own computer, in a file only our Windows user can read. We never see or store passwords.
  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): our own account's display name, username and identifier, shown so we can confirm which account a video will be posted to.
  • Posting (video.publish): the video file and caption we chose are sent to TikTok to publish on our own account after we confirm the post.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data about other people.
  • We do not access other users' accounts, followers' data or messages.
  • We do not use data received from TikTok for advertising or profiling.

4. Storage and security

All data stays on our own computer, outside cloud-synced folders, except what we deliberately publish to our accounts. Tokens can be revoked at any time in the platform's app-permission settings.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when we disconnect the account or revoke access. Records of our own published posts are kept for internal reporting.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Contact Never Fail Media through @NeverFailMedia on TikTok, YouTube or Instagram.
